# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Developers, architects, and platform engineers exploring the GDG Cloud Bengaluru community and the next event. They want a clear sense of what the community offers, who it is for, and how to join or register fast.

## Product Purpose
Present the GDG Cloud Bengaluru developer community with credibility and momentum, spotlight the upcoming event as the primary call to action, and guide visitors to register with minimal friction.

## Brand Personality
Confident, precise, welcoming, modern. The tone should feel premium and engineering-led, with clarity over hype.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS or crypto landing templates, heavy gradients as decoration, and endless identical card grids that flatten hierarchy.

## Design Principles
- Lead with the next event, then reinforce credibility with concrete community scale and past moments.
- Show practical value: clear tracks, dates, and outcomes over buzzwords.
- Premium clarity: strong hierarchy, careful spacing, and high contrast for effortless scanning.
- Community warmth through real imagery and human moments.
- Frictionless conversion: obvious CTAs and clean paths to register or learn more.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast and keyboard-friendly interactions, with reduced-motion friendly transitions.
